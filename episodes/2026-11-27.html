    # DoD's Commercial-App Bet and the VA Warning Label


    Hegseth's military pay modernization memo names a real, near-term procurement appetite for cloud-native, app-driven platforms — but the VA EHR program, which made the identical upstream bet on commercial software, is now a $16.9 billion cautionary tale. Today's episode maps the bifurcated procurement lanes opening in military pay, explains why the VA EHR's apparent facility-level progress is not the stabilization signal it appears to be, and draws the contract-boundary lesson that separates priceable FFP work from scope traps dressed in commercial-item language.
