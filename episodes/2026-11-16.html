    # The Vehicle Architecture Is the Weapon


    DoD is institutionalizing AI urgency — rebranding CDAO, operationalizing agentic tools, absorbing niche capability through prime M&A — while the acquisition vehicles that would let innovative small firms compete for the resulting contracts remain structurally broken. Today's C-UAS procurement critique is the sharpest concrete articulation of that gap yet. For small FFP AI shops, this is not a trend to monitor; it is the market structure you are already operating inside.
