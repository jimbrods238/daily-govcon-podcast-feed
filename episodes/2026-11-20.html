    # GAO's Fifteen-Year Question Mark


    The Trump administration's nomination of an OMB insider to lead GAO for a 15-year term is the most consequential structural story in small-business contracting right now. For small FFP firms that rely on GAO bid protests as their primary — and often only affordable — check on agency discretion, a politicized protest adjudication body changes the enforcement calculus fundamentally. Today's episode also traces why the federal AI market is bifurcating between a stalled application layer and an accelerating compliance-and-infrastructure layer, and why the Oracle EHRM hearing is better read as an incumbent-entrenchment signal than a challenger opportunity.
