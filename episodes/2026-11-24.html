    # Rhombus Power and the End of Pilot Tolerance


    Federal AI has crossed from a regime where pilots were graded on effort to one where contracts are graded on delivered mission outcomes — and Rhombus Power is the first visible casualty. For every small AI shop still selling on demo, the accountability ratchet is now a legal and reputational risk, not a theoretical one. The correct response is not to dial back capability claims; it is to build the measurement infrastructure that makes those claims provable.
