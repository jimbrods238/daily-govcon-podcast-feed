    # GSA's LLM Rule Is Already in Effect — Reprice Before Award


    GSA's new 'Basic Safeguarding of Data within Large Language Model Artificial Intelligence Systems' rule is the first binding compliance layer targeting how AI contractors handle government data inside models — and the contractual risk now lives in your statement of work, not the fine print. For small FFP-preferring AI shops, the action item is immediate: map your model training, fine-tuning, and inference pipeline against the rule's permitted-use list before your next proposal goes out the door. Discovering that exposure mid-performance on a fixed-price contract is an unrecoverable position.
