    # DISA's Manufactured Urgency


    DISA's sole-source expansion of Leidos' Defense Enclave Services contract is the sharpest example yet of a broader pattern: agencies manufacturing deadline pressure to justify locking incumbents in and locking small businesses out. A GAO protest filed October 8th by an SDVOSB with a competing vehicle puts that pattern on the record — and the mid-January decision could set precedent that constrains the tactic across DoD IT procurement.
