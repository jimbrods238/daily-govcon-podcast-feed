    # DISA's Sole-Source Trap and the Selection Filter


    The federal government is simultaneously signaling openness to new vendors and systematically locking them out — and today's DISA Enclave protest makes the mechanism explicit. A small SDVOSB is challenging DISA's sole-source expansion to Leidos after the agency abandoned a small-business sources sought notice, sat on the requirement for three years, and then invoked deadline pressure to justify skipping competition. The broader pattern: AI governance hardening, defense tech M&A, and compressed agency timelines are all narrowing the funnel — in ways that favor clean-provenance U.S. AI shops that know how to protest.
