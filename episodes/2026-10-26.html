    # Pentagon Turns Political AI Directives Into Contract Law


    The Defense Department's new ideological neutrality guidance for AI procurement isn't political noise — it's a signed memorandum with mandatory compliance teeth, standardized contract language, and a new audit surface that creates real performance risk on firm-fixed-price work. For small AI shops with auditable, domestically-trained models, this guidance is a differentiator, not a burden. Meanwhile, the federal AI market is bifurcating: sole-source incumbents at the foundation-model layer, a crowded commercial marketplace below — and three agencies are each trying to impose order on the same broken architecture without coordinating.
