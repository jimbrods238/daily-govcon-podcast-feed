    # The White House Installs Itself Between Contractors and Congress


    A new executive order requires federal contractors to route all Congressional responses through the White House and their contracting agencies — backed by the threat of termination and suspension. The order lands as Democrats prepare to take the House in January with federal contracts as a top investigative target, and it may function less as a contractor compliance burden than as a protective shield for large incumbents already under Congressional scrutiny. Meanwhile, DoD's consolidation of cyber governance and the Army's NGC2 data-foundation architecture are tightening the hub-and-spoke picture of executive control over federal AI and technology contracting.
