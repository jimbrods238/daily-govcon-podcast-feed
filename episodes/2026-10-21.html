    # AI Compliance Goes Binding While the Market Closes


    GSA's AI class deviation is now enforceable, not aspirational — and it arrived the same week HHS sole-sourced to Anthropic and Google launched Gemini for government. The foundation-model layer is consolidating fast, compliance costs just rose for anyone on GSA vehicles, and the durable opportunity for small AI shops is in the instrumentation and integration layer above the locked-in platforms, not in competing for the base model relationship.
