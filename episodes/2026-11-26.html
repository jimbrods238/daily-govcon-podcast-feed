    # Civil Agencies Enter the AI Buy


    FEMA's new fraud-detection AI RFI — responses due December 4 — is the clearest signal yet that federal AI procurement is no longer a DoD-exclusive market. Narrow-scope, commercially available AI buys are spreading to civil agencies for budget-defensive reasons, and that's the most favorable structural signal for small FFP AI shops in two weeks. Meanwhile, the third consecutive year of declining GAO protests is probably not a sign of a healthier procurement environment — it's a sign that small contractors are swallowing losses and moving on, which makes pre-award vehicle positioning the only viable risk strategy.
