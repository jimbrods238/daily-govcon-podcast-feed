    # MAPS Swallows Another Protest Wave — CMMC Breaks the Vehicle


    The Army's $50 billion MAPS professional services vehicle has absorbed a third round of protests, this time targeting both small-business access architecture and CMMC requirements that DOD itself has partially paused. The episode argues that CMMC mandates dropped into vehicle solicitations without tiered compliance pathways are now breaking vehicles before a single task order issues — a materially worse outcome than the offeror-elimination dynamic this show covered on November 3rd. For small FFP-preferring AI shops, the MAPS delay makes SEWP VI the clearest near-term on-ramp, and the counter-narrative cautions against reading federal workforce cuts as a universal FFP opportunity.
