    # Pentagon Sorts Its Portfolio: Funded, Frozen, and Flying Blind


    The Pentagon's formal request for CR anomaly carve-outs is not a budget story — it's an acquisition architecture story. When DoD triage-funds Golden Dome and named priorities through anomalies while everything else flatlines at prior-year rates, it bifurcates the entire contractor market for the next twelve to eighteen months. Small FFP AI shops that haven't explicitly mapped their programs to anomaly-protected priorities should treat their FY27 pipeline as speculative until Congress acts.
