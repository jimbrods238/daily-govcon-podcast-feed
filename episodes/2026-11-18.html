    # Deregulation's Hidden Asterisk: DoD's Transparency Trap


    DoD is publicly pursuing acquisition deregulation while simultaneously demanding deeper contractor cost and pricing data — a combination that shifts negotiating leverage toward the government on recompetes and erodes the margin protection that FFP structure normally provides. For small AI shops, the Army's open-range prototyping push is real, but the transition from prototype to funded follow-on now runs directly into a tightening cost-transparency regime that can weaponize your own historical pricing against you.
