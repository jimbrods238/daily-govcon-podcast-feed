    # AI Evaluations Are Live — The Rules Aren't


    Industry and legal experts are formally petitioning the FAR Council to set binding standards for AI use in bid evaluations — a gap exposed by the TRAX International Court of Federal Claims case and made urgent by the FAR overhaul's October 19th comment deadline. The governance vacuum isn't closing fast: formal rules are 18-24 months away at minimum, meaning the protest environment will be shaped by litigation before it's shaped by rulemaking. For small AI contractors, the move is to start building protest-record documentation now.
