    # Gatekeeper Goes Live — And Closes the Door


    The Army's Project Gatekeeper launched Friday, automating the ATO process that has historically taken months — but the consensus that this is unambiguously good for small vendors is wrong. Gatekeeper rewards architectures already designed for automated compliance, and the Army's plan to integrate frontier AI models into the authorization pipeline creates a path-of-least-resistance that favors large incumbents. For a small FFP-preferring AI shop, the compliance barrier hasn't been removed — it's been moved earlier in the acquisition timeline, before the solicitation drops.
