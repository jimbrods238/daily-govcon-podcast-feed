    # DISA's ICAM OTA: Zero Trust Gets a Deadline


    DISA's commercial solutions opening for a next-generation enterprise ICAM prototype — scoped to IL5 and IL6, deadline September 2027 — is the most concrete zero-trust acquisition signal in months. Combined with CISA's structural capacity collapse and the False Claims Act outpacing CMMC enforcement, today's episode argues that identity infrastructure is now a durable, fixed-scope funding lane and that the competitive moat belongs to whoever owns the enterprise platform layer, not the point-solution integrators circling it.
